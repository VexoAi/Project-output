# 🌿 BioBuddy Biology Curriculum & Syllabus Guide

## 10 Core Biological Domains & Learning Objectives

### 1. 🧬 DNA & Genetics
- **Core Concepts**: Deoxyribonucleic Acid structure, double helix antiparallel strands, nitrogenous bases ($A, T, C, G$).
- **Complementary Pairing**: Adenine pairs with Thymine (2 H-bonds); Cytosine pairs with Guanine (3 H-bonds).
- **Mendelian Inheritance**: Genotypes (Homozygous Dominant $BB$, Heterozygous $Bb$, Homozygous Recessive $bb$) and Phenotypic expression.

### 2. 🫀 Human Circulatory System
- **Anatomy**: 4 chambers (Right Atrium, Right Ventricle, Left Atrium, Left Ventricle), Aorta, Superior/Inferior Vena Cava, Pulmonary vessels.
- **Pathways**: Pulmonary circulation (oxygenation in lungs) vs Systemic circulation (body delivery).
- **Cardiac Cycle**: Systole (contraction) vs Diastole (filling), valve operations (Tricuspid, Bicuspid/Mitral).

### 3. 🫁 Respiratory System
- **Breathing Mechanics**: Inhalation via diaphragm contraction ($P \propto 1/V$ Boyle's law) and exhalation.
- **Gas Exchange**: Partial pressure gradients driving $O_2$ diffusion from alveoli to capillaries and $CO_2$ removal.

### 4. 🧠 Human Nervous System
- **Central Nervous System (CNS)**: Cerebrum (thought & sensory cortex), Cerebellum (balance & fine motor), Brainstem (vital autonomic control).
- **Neurotransmission**: Action potential propagation along myelinated axons and neurotransmitter vesicle release across synaptic clefts.

### 5. 🌱 Photosynthesis
- **Chemical Equation**: $6CO_2 + 6H_2O + \text{Photons} \xrightarrow{\text{Chlorophyll}} C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2$.
- **Two Stages**: Light-dependent reactions (Thylakoids) and Calvin Cycle (Stroma).

### 6. 🔬 Cell Structure & Organelles
- **Eukaryotic Organelles**: Nucleus, Mitochondria, Endoplasmic Reticulum (Rough & Smooth), Golgi Apparatus, Cell Membrane.
- **Plant vs Animal Distinctions**: Cellulose cell walls, chloroplasts, and large central vacuoles in plants.

### 7. 🦠 Microorganisms
- **Three Microbe Domains**: Bacteria (prokaryotic rods/cocci), Viruses (acellular protein capsid with RNA/DNA), Fungi (chitin walls, hyphae/spores).
- **Pharmacology & Immunology**: Antibiotic zones of inhibition and white blood cell macrophage phagocytosis.

### 8. 🍎 Human Digestive System
- **Gastrointestinal Tract**: Mouth (salivary amylase) $\rightarrow$ Esophagus (peristalsis) $\rightarrow$ Stomach (pepsin & HCl acid pH 1.5) $\rightarrow$ Small Intestine (villi nutrient absorption) $\rightarrow$ Large Intestine (water reabsorption).

### 9. 🐾 Animal Classification
- **Vertebrates (5 Classes)**: Mammals, Birds, Reptiles, Amphibians, Fish.
- **Invertebrates**: Arthropods, Mollusks, Annelids.
- **Diagnostic Traits**: Thermoregulation (Endothermic vs Ectothermic), integument (hair, feathers, scales, skin), and reproduction.

### 10. 🌍 Ecosystem & Food Chain
- **Trophic Energy Flow**: Producers $\rightarrow$ Primary Consumers $\rightarrow$ Secondary Consumers $\rightarrow$ Apex Predators with the 10% energy transfer rule.
- **Population Dynamics**: Predator-prey cyclic equilibrium and decomposer nutrient recycling.
